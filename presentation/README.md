# 📑 Business Analysis Presentation

This folder contains the executive presentation for the **Sales Performance & Forecasting Analysis** project.

## Presentation Focus

The presentation translates the Excel analysis into a management-focused business story:

- Business problem and management questions
- Executive performance overview
- 6P business diagnostic framework
- Customer, product, regional, and shipping performance
- Business risks and root-cause hypotheses
- 2023 revenue forecasting
- Forecast backtesting and MAPE validation
- SWOT analysis
- Recommendations and corrective actions
- Management decision support

## Analytical Approach

**Business Problem → Evidence → Risk → Root-Cause Hypothesis → Diagnostic Analysis → Corrective Action → Decision Support**

> The presentation demonstrates how analytical findings were translated into actionable management recommendations.
